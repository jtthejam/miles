Free prices from Google Flights (no key, uses the date grid)
  1. Drag Send to Miles Maximiser to your bookmarks bar (once).
  2. Tap G on a row. Google Flights opens filtered to that airline, cabin and your dates.
  3. Optionally open Date grid to compare nearby dates, then click the bookmark. The cheapest price within your dates (± flexibility) is saved to the row.

The bookmark only reads the prices shown on the page and opens this app with them; it sends nothing elsewhere. If Google changes its page, it may stop working until the app is updated. Keep the airline filter set to one airline: miles depend on who flies you.

Sharing with other users (anonymised)

Shared files keep only route, cabin, the dates priced, the price, where it came from and the day it was searched. Your search ranges, search times and ids are left out, each date pair is merged to one price per day, and the order is shuffled.

Prices are shared only within the private data repo: Google Flights and SerpApi results aren't licensed for public redistribution.
